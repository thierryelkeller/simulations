GeoLogo

Interactive Geometric Engine

How to use GeoLogo

  • 1. Draw: Select Line, Rect, or Circle. Click and drag on the grid.
  • 2. Select: Click the 'Select' tool, then click any shape you drew. It will highlight yellow.
  • 3. Transform: Once selected, a Properties Panel appears on the left. Use it to precisely Rotate, Translate, or Reflect the shape.
  • 4. Clone: Use the Clone button to easily make parallel lines or repeated shapes.
