How to use GeoLogo

  • 1. Magnetic Grid: The cursor snaps automatically for precise alignments.
  • 2. Zoom: Scroll your mouse wheel or use the (+/-) buttons to zoom in and out.
  • 3. Transform & Preview: Select a shape. Type an Angle or Translation. A ghost preview will appear! Click 'Apply'.
  • 4. History Report: The right panel tracks all shapes and their transformations automatically.
