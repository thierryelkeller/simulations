How to use GeoLogo

  • 1. Magnetic Grid: The cursor snaps automatically for precise alignments.
  • 2. Transform & Preview: Select a shape. Type an Angle or X/Y Translation. A ghost preview will appear! Click 'Apply' to confirm.
  • 3. Style: Pick your colors, gradients, and direction. Changing them while a shape is selected updates it instantly.
