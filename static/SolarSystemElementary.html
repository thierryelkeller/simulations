Ancestral Star Knowledge

For thousands of years, the Anishinaabe, Ininiw (Cree), and Dakota peoples of the prairies have used the night sky as a calendar, map, and storybook. The movement of the stars and planets indicates when to plant, hunt, and hold ceremonies.
