Solar System Trajectory

Welcome to the cosmic sandbox. This interactive application is designed to visualize the true motion of the Solar System as it travels through the universe.

Standard orbital models often depict the planets circling a stationary Sun. However, in reality, the Sun is moving at an astonishing speed of approximately 220 km/s around the galactic center. When you combine this forward momentum with planetary orbits, it creates a magnificent helical (corkscrew) trajectory through space.

How to Use the Simulations
